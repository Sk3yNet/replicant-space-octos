Replicant Space wallpaper 1.4.0

To show your galaxy and systems here:
  1. Open your Replicant Space web client and go to Account › Desktop wallpaper.
  2. Press Create wallpaper link and copy the link it shows.
  3. In the Octos app, open this wallpaper's settings and paste it into Wallpaper link.
The link looks like https://your-server/wallpaper/…/#key=rsw_….
